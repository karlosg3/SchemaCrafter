# Anti-Cliché Color Palettes for Indie & Open-Source Tools

Curated, timeless color systems inspired by Swiss graphic design, architectural drafting, industrial hardware, and modern indie developer tools—deliberately avoiding generic dark-purple/cyan AI templates.

## 1. Editorial Paper & Terracotta
- **Background / Paper:** `#F5F0E6` (matte warm cream)
- **Primary Ink:** `#1A1918` (deep warm charcoal)
- **Accent:** `#D95D39` (burnt terracotta)
- **Best for:** Pure typographic monograms (`SC.`, `DB.`), notebooks, writing/documentation tools, warm minimalist studios.
- **UI Derivation:** Light mode on bone paper (`#F7F3EC`) with dark ink; Dark mode on warm espresso-stone (`#181615`) with terracotta focus highlights.

## 2. Nordic Forest & Warm Ochre
- **Background:** `#112A22` (deep pine forest)
- **Primary Mark:** `#F4EFE6` (warm ivory)
- **Accent:** `#E9A84C` (warm ochre / brass) or `#34D399` (muted sage-emerald)
- **Best for:** Modular / interlocking "crafter" marks, long-session creative tools where low eye-strain matters.
- **UI Derivation:** Deep slate-pine surfaces, ivory typography, ochre primary-key / highlight accents.

## 3. Architectural Prussian Blueprint & Vermilion
- **Background Gradient:** `#265385` → `#193A62` → `#102644` (or flat `#152B47`)
- **Primary Mark:** `#FCF9F2` → `#EFE8DA` (warm drafting ivory)
- **Accent:** `#EA6A38` / `#F57B47` → `#DA5524` (warm vermilion-orange)
- **Best for:** Technical diagramming, database/API design tools, CAD/blueprint aesthetics that feel trustworthy yet warm and inviting.
- **UI Derivation:** Navy-slate blueprint surfaces in dark mode, warm drafting-paper light mode, crisp vermilion accent for active links and primary actions.

## 4. Terracotta Beta & Deep Navy
- **Background Gradient:** `#E8633B` → `#D64A25` → `#B9391A`
- **Primary Mark:** `#FFFBF5` → `#F3EADF` (warm alabaster)
- **Accent / Secondary:** `#234873` → `#132A47` (deep Prussian navy)
- **Best for:** Energetic open-source tools, preview/beta channel builds (reminiscent of classic blueprint beta badges), bold desktop dock presence.

## 5. Vibrant Xcode Blueprint Blue
- **Background Gradient:** `#56BEFF` → `#2A88FF` → `#1461EC`
- **Primary Mark:** `#FFFDF9` → `#F1ECE2` (crisp white/ivory) or `#1A2230` (sculpted obsidian)
- **Accent:** `#FF7A45` → `#E2511E` (vermilion) or `#FFFFFF`
- **Best for:** Developer IDEs, compilers, and native desktop developer utilities aiming for unmistakable developer-tool recognition.
